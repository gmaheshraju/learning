About Heshvi

Made with love for Heshvitha, and shared for every little learner. The games follow what research says helps at each age: open-ended play, puzzles, patterns, planning ahead, and lots of talking together. English words come with Telugu hints.

Bujji’s voice was recorded with Kokoro, an open voice model (Apache 2.0). Stickers, progress and settings never leave this phone.
